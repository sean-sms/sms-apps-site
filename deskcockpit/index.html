    Features

    Made for staying on task.

    ✦

    Glow and pin

    Make the two or three things that matter today glow, and pin the ones that should always be there.

    ◐

    Lost Focus

    Untouched cards move to a Lost Focus section after 10 days and leave after another 35. Pinned and glowing cards never move. Change the timing, or switch it off.

    Files show what they are and links show their site's icon and page title, so you know what's behind a card at a glance.

    ☰

    Glowing and pinned cards are a click away from the menu bar, and from a desktop widget in three sizes.

    Open a card, add a link, or list what's glowing with the Shortcuts app.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Does Desk Cockpit move or change my files?

    No. A card is a shortcut to a file or link. Removing a card, or a card moving to Lost Focus, never touches the file itself.

    What happens to a card in Lost Focus?

    It sits in a collapsed Lost Focus section at the bottom of the board. Open it, Quick Look it or move it to a section and it's back in play. If you leave it alone for a further 35 days, the card is removed. The file behind it is never affected.

    Can I change the timing, or turn Lost Focus off?

    Yes. Settings lets you choose how many days of rest send a card to Lost Focus, how long it stays there, or switch it off completely.

    Which Macs does it run on?

    Any Mac running macOS 26 or later.

    Is my board backed up?

    Desk Cockpit takes a dated backup the first time you open it each day, and before any import. You can export a copy, or restore one, from Settings.
